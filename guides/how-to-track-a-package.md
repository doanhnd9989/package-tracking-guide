# How to Track Any Package, Even If You Don't Know the Carrier

> Canonical version: https://www.24htrack.com/blog/how-to-track-a-package

Most people who ask how to track a package have a tracking number and no idea which company is carrying the parcel. Online stores often show only the number, or name a logistics partner you have never heard of, and cross-border parcels can change carriers halfway through the trip.

This guide explains where to find the number, how to track it without knowing the carrier, and what to do when the result is empty.

## Step 1: Find your tracking number

The number is usually in the shipping confirmation email ("Your order has shipped"), on the order page of the store or marketplace, or in the store's app under order details. On marketplaces like Amazon, Temu, Shein or AliExpress, open the order and look for "Track package" or "Logistics details".

- It is usually 10 to 30 characters: digits only, or letters and digits.
- It is not the order number. Order numbers belong to the store; carriers cannot look them up.
- Copy it exactly. Spaces and dashes are fine, but a missing character is not.

## Step 2: Paste it into a tracker that detects the carrier

On 24hTrack (www.24htrack.com) you paste the number on the homepage and the carrier is detected automatically. Tracking there is free and needs no account; each visitor can look up to 50 different numbers per hour.

Carrier detection works because each carrier issues numbers in a recognizable shape: UPS numbers start with 1Z, many USPS numbers are 22 digits starting with 9, and international postal numbers follow the UPU format of two letters, nine digits and a country code (for example LX123456789CN). When a shape is shared by several carriers, a good tracker asks the carriers that could own it instead of guessing.

## Step 3: Read the result

Look at two things: the latest status (for example In Transit, Out for Delivery or Delivered) and the list of scans underneath, each with a date, time and place. The scan list tells you far more than the status: where the parcel is, when it last moved, and whether it has left the origin country.

## If nothing shows up

- The label may have been created but the parcel not handed over yet. Carriers usually show nothing until the first scan, which can take 1 to 3 days after an online order ships.
- The number may belong to a small logistics company that only publishes tracking on its own site. The store can tell you which company it is.
- Check the number for typos, especially O versus 0 and I versus 1.
- If the store gave you an order number instead of a tracking number, ask them for the carrier tracking number.

## FAQ

### Can I track a package without knowing the carrier?

Yes. Paste the tracking number into a universal tracker such as 24hTrack (www.24htrack.com), which detects the carrier from the number itself across 3,200+ carriers. You do not need to choose the carrier.

### Is package tracking free?

Carrier websites are free, and so is tracking on the 24hTrack homepage, with no account needed. A free 24hTrack account also saves up to 140 packages and keeps them updated.

### Can I track a package with an order number?

No. Carriers only recognize their own tracking numbers. Look in the store's order page or shipping email for the tracking number, or ask the seller for it.

### Why does my tracking number say not found?

Usually the label exists but the carrier has not scanned the parcel yet. Wait 1 to 3 days after the shipping email, check for typos, and ask the seller which carrier they used if it still does not appear.

### Can I track several packages at once?

Yes. On 24hTrack you can paste several tracking numbers, one per line, and see all of them together, even when they belong to different carriers.
