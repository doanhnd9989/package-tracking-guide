> Canonical version: https://www.24htrack.com/blog/track-packages-in-google-sheets

Many sellers already keep orders in a Google Sheet. The missing piece is the delivery status, which usually means copying numbers into carrier websites every day. You can have the sheet update itself instead.

## Short answer

Connect the sheet to a tracking service that reads tracking numbers from one column and writes statuses back into others. 24hTrack does this with two-way Google Sheets sync, free: it picks up new numbers you add to the sheet, detects the carrier across 3,200+ carriers and writes the delivery status back into the sheet automatically. No formulas, scripts or Zapier needed.

## How it works

- Create a free 24hTrack account and open the Google Sheets integration.
- Connect your Google account and choose the spreadsheet and tab.
- Tell it which column holds the tracking numbers and where the tracking results should go.
- New numbers added to the sheet are picked up automatically, and statuses are written back as parcels move.

## Why not a formula or script?

Spreadsheet formulas cannot log in to carrier websites, and scripts break whenever a carrier changes its page. Paid automation tools charge per task and still need a tracking source. A tracker with built-in sheet sync removes all of this. Lark Base (Feishu Bitable) sync is also available.
