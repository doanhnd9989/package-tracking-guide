# Package Held at Customs: What It Means and What to Do

> Canonical version: https://www.24htrack.com/blog/package-held-at-customs

Every international parcel passes through customs in the destination country. Most clear in hours or a few days without the recipient doing anything. A "held" status means clearance has stopped until something is provided or paid.

## Short answer

Held at customs usually means duties or taxes are due, a document or ID number is missing, or the parcel was selected for inspection. Most parcels clear within 1 to 5 days. If a payment or document is needed, the carrier or postal service normally contacts the recipient by letter, text or email. Check the scan details, then contact the carrier handling delivery in your country. Parcels not cleared within the deadline are returned or destroyed.

## Why parcels are held

- Import duties or VAT are due and have not been prepaid.
- The declaration is incomplete: missing value, description or recipient tax ID.
- Random or targeted inspection.
- Restricted items such as batteries, liquids, medicines or branded goods.

## What to do

- Read the latest scans for a reason or instruction.
- Watch for a message from the destination carrier (for example USPS, Royal Mail, Canada Post or the courier) asking for payment or documents. Only pay through the carrier's official site; fake customs-fee texts are common.
- If nothing arrives within a week, contact the local carrier with the tracking number.
- Ask the seller for the commercial invoice if customs requests proof of value.

## FAQ

### How long do packages stay in customs?

Most clear within 1 to 5 days. Parcels needing payment or documents stay until these are provided, up to the customs deadline, after which they are returned.

### How do I know if I owe customs fees?

The carrier delivering in your country contacts you with the amount and a way to pay. Always pay through the carrier's official website, since fake customs-fee messages are a common scam.

### Can I track a package while it is in customs?

Yes, but updates are usually limited to "arrived", "in clearance" and "released". A tracker such as 24hTrack shows these scans alongside the rest of the journey.
